SimMorph Studio v1.6 - CAS Bridge (experimental)

1. Open The Sims 4 and enter Create-a-Sim, with the face preset panel open.
2. In SimMorph Studio click Capture CAS Position.
3. Within 4 seconds, switch to Sims 4 and hover the mouse over the preset tile you want to click. Do not click.
4. When the countdown finishes, press Ctrl+Alt+C with Sims 4 as the active window.
5. SimMorph Studio sends exactly one mouse click at the captured screen position.

IMPORTANT: This is an experimental one-click bridge, NOT automatic face selection or sculpting. It does not know which CAS preset corresponds to M1-M19/F1-F19, nor detect whether CAS is open. The current app's photo validation does not yet provide facial landmarks or automatic morphing.

Use windowed/borderless Sims 4 with stable UI scale. The hotkey may conflict with another program. Never use the hotkey outside Sims 4. If the app is elevated and Sims 4 is not, input may behave differently. Logs: %LOCALAPPDATA%\\SimMorphStudio\\Logs\\app.log
