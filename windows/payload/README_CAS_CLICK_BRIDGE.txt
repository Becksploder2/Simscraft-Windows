SimMorph Studio v3.9 - CAS Click Bridge (experimental)

This is a real, LIMITED in-game control bridge, NOT automatic photo-based face morphing.

1. Run The Sims 4 in windowed/borderless mode and enter CAS.
2. In SimMorph Studio click Set CAS Click Point.
3. Within four seconds, switch to Sims 4 and hover over a desired CAS preset/control. Keep the mouse still.
4. Return to SimMorph Studio. Click Apply One CAS Click.
5. Within five seconds switch to Sims 4 and keep CAS visible.
6. SimMorph Studio will send exactly ONE mouse click at your saved point if Sims 4 is foreground. It then takes a fresh screenshot and updates the preview.

IMPORTANT: Clicking an incorrect control may change an unintended CAS setting. You are responsible for choosing the point. The screenshot confirms capture, not a verified facial change. There is no MediaPipe engine or automatic morph matching bundled.

For safety the app does not click if Sims 4 is not the foreground window.
